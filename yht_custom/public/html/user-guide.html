Kathoom Alkhobar — Branch User Guide

Everything a branch operator needs, in the order you will actually need it. Written against the live system, so what you read here is what you will see on screen. If the screen disagrees with this page, trust the screen and tell us.

Signing in & what you can see

Your login is tied to one branch. Everything follows from that.

Your lists are already filtered. You are not looking at the whole company. A list shows your branch's documents only, so a total on your screen is your branch's total. Cancelled documents are hidden from you entirely — if a document has vanished rather than gone red, it was cancelled.
Screens are deliberately shorter than standard ERPNext. Cost centre, Project, Currency and the Currency-and-Price-List block are hidden because this business does not use them — one company, one currency, no project accounting. Nothing is missing; it is filled in behind the scenes.

Your dashboard

You land on the Branch Dashboard when you sign in. It is your home screen and it is built for one job: see where the branch stands, then start work in one click.

The five numbers along the top

Overdue is the one to watch. Outstanding includes invoices that are not late yet, so it always looks large. Overdue is the part that is genuinely late. Big figures are shortened — 1.4M means 1.4 million riyals. For the exact amount, open the report.

Drafts

The row of grey chips under the cards counts your unsubmitted documents. Click one to open just those drafts. A large Quotation number is normal — quotations accumulate.

Quick Actions and Reports

Tiles, not menus. Sales Invoice opens a new blank invoice straight away; every other tile opens that document's list, where you can browse or press + Add.

Needs Attention

Three live columns at the bottom — Unpaid Invoices, Orders To Deliver and Delivery Notes To Bill. Each shows the oldest few with a count of the rest. A red bar down the left of an unpaid invoice means it is past its due date. Click any number to open that document.

Delivery Notes To Bill is your daily safety net. Anything sitting there is stock that has left the branch and has not been invoiced yet. That column should be short by the end of the day.

Getting back to the dashboard

Two ways, both always available, from any screen:

  • Click ← Dashboard in the top bar, to the right of the search box.
  • Click the Yaser Hameed logo in the top-left corner.
You do not need the browser Back button. Either route above returns you cleanly. Browser Back can drop you onto a half-loaded screen or a form you had started editing.

Quotation

A price offer. It commits nothing and moves no stock.

  1. Dashboard → Quotation → + Add Quotation.
  2. Pick the Party (the customer).
  3. Add each item as a row: item, quantity, rate. The rate fills in from the price list — see Deciding the price before you change it.
  4. Save, then Submit when you are ready to send it.
  5. Print to hand over or email.

Number format: KSSQ-26-0001. When the customer accepts, use Create → Sales Order from the submitted quotation rather than retyping it.

Sales Order

A confirmed commitment to supply. Still no stock movement, but the order now shows on the dashboard under Orders To Deliver until it is delivered.

  1. Best route: open the accepted Quotation → Create → Sales Order.
  2. Or Dashboard → Sales Order → + Add Sales Order and enter it fresh.
  3. Check quantities and the delivery date, then Submit.
Stock is reserved when you submit. Stock reservation is switched on, so a submitted order holds that quantity against your warehouse. It stops two people promising the same box to two customers.

Number format: KSSO-26-0001. The Print As field lets you print the same document titled Sales Order, Quotation or Proforma Invoice — the document itself does not change, only the printed heading.

Deliver first, then bill

This is the single most important rule in the system. Stock leaves the branch on a Delivery Note. A Sales Invoice only bills what has already left. One movement, one record — never both on one document.

Standard ERPNext lets you tick Update Stock on an invoice and move stock straight from there. On this system that is switched off on purpose. If you try, the invoice refuses to save and tells you why:

Stock is delivered on a Delivery Note. Create the Delivery Note first, then bill it.

The reason is that it kept the stock ledger and the sales ledger honest. When stock and money move on the same document, a correction to one silently corrupts the other.

Step 1 — the Delivery Note

  1. From the submitted Sales Order: Create → Delivery Note. Items, quantities and rates carry over.
  2. No order? Dashboard → Delivery Note → + Add Delivery Note.
  3. Check the quantity actually going out. Change it here if it differs — this is the record of what physically left.
  4. Submit. Stock leaves Stores - KATC at this moment.
  5. Print — the format prints a customer copy and an office copy with a signature block.
A submitted Delivery Note is locked. You cannot edit it afterwards, and a delivery line cannot carry a zero rate. Get the quantity right before you submit. If it is wrong after submission, ask your manager — it needs a return document, not an edit.

Step 2 — the Sales Invoice

  1. Open the submitted Delivery Note → Create → Sales Invoice.
  2. The items arrive already linked to the delivery. Do not retype them.
  3. Set Payment Mode — see Cash or credit.
  4. Check Payment Due Date, which fills in from the customer's payment terms.
  5. Submit.
Always start from the Delivery Note. Building the invoice from the delivery keeps the two linked, so Delivery Notes To Bill on your dashboard clears correctly. An invoice typed from scratch leaves the delivery sitting in that column forever, looking unbilled.

Deciding the price

You are never guessing. The system already knows what this item sold for, to this customer and to everyone else. Two buttons sit under the items table, next to Add Row:

Price Assist

Click into the item row you are pricing, then press Price Assist. It answers:

  • Price list rate — the standard rate from Kathoom Selling Price
  • Last rate to this customer — with the date and invoice number
  • Last rate to anyone — with which customer
  • Recent range — the low, high and average across recent sales
  • Valuation and margin at your rate — red if you are selling below cost
  • Stock — what is available in your warehouse right now

Two shortcuts in the dialog: Use price list rate and Use last rate to this customer fill the row for you.

Show Price History

The last 30 sales of that item — date, customer, quantity, rate, invoice. Use it when a customer says "you charged me less last time". The answer is on screen in one click.

You will get a quiet nudge. Type a rate that differs from the last rate to that customer and a short message appears saying how far above or below it is. It does not block you — it just makes sure you meant it.
Margin in red means you are selling below what the stock cost. Sometimes that is a deliberate decision. It should never be an accident.

Cash or credit

Every Sales Invoice carries a Payment Mode, right under the payment due date. It has two values and it is a real decision, not a formality.

Payment ModeMeansWhat happens
Credit
the default		 The customer will pay later Invoice is submitted and the amount joins their outstanding balance. You will see it on the dashboard until it is paid.
Cash You are collecting now As soon as you submit, the Collect Payment dialog opens so you can record what was tendered.
The trap: leaving it on Credit for a cash sale. The invoice submits perfectly happily. The money is in the drawer and the system still thinks the customer owes it, so somebody chases a customer who has already paid. Set Payment Mode before you submit.

The customer's position, before you commit

Pick the customer and two indicators appear at the top of the invoice:

  • Outstanding — everything they already owe. Orange if it is above zero.
  • Credit limit — shown only if finance has set one for that customer. Red if this sale would take them over it.

Going over the limit produces a warning, not a block. You can proceed. It exists so the decision is made with eyes open.

Collecting the money

The Collect Payment dialog opens automatically after you submit a Cash invoice. You can also reopen it any time from the Collect Payment button on a submitted, unpaid invoice.

  1. Amount Due is shown at the top and cannot be edited.
  2. Enter what was actually tendered against each payment mode. Cash modes are listed first.
  3. Split it freely — part cash, part card, part transfer. The running Tendered and Still due totals update as you type.
  4. Press Record Payment. One receipt is created per mode used.

You cannot tender more than is due. Over-tendering is refused rather than creating a credit you would have to unpick later. Give change from the drawer, not from the system.

Returns & credit notes

Goods coming back reverse the same two steps, in reverse order.

  1. Open the original submitted Sales Invoice → Create → Return / Credit Note.
  2. Set the quantities actually returned. Rates mirror the original, including any zero lines.
  3. Submit. The credit note takes its own number, KSCN-26-….
  4. Stock coming physically back needs a return delivery note, KSDRN-26-….
A credit note never opens the payment dialog. On a credit note the money moves outward, so tendering makes no sense. If you are refunding cash, ask your manager to raise a payment against the credit note.

Receiving goods

Buying mirrors selling: stock arrives on a Purchase Receipt, and the Purchase Invoice bills what already arrived.

  1. Dashboard → Purchase Receipt → + Add.
  2. Supplier, then the items and quantities actually received into Stores - KATC.
  3. Submit. Stock arrives at this moment.
  4. Then Create → Purchase Invoice from the receipt to book the supplier's bill.
Receive what actually arrived, not what was ordered. Negative stock is not allowed on this system, so a receipt entered short and corrected later will block the sale in between. Count first.

Recording an expense bill

For costs with nothing to put in stock — rent, fuel, a repair, a phone bill. It is a Purchase Invoice with the Is Expense Invoice box ticked.

  1. Dashboard → Purchase Invoice → + Add.
  2. Tick Is Expense Invoice. The form changes shape.
  3. Pick the Expense Head — the account this cost belongs to.
  4. Add a row per line on the supplier's bill: a description and an amount. No item code.
  5. Enter the supplier's own invoice number in Supplier Invoice No.
  6. Paid on the spot in cash? Tick Is Paid and choose the account it came out of.
  7. Submit. It takes an expense number, KSEXP-26-….
The trap: putting a stock item on an expense invoice. It is refused. If what you bought goes into stock, it is not an expense — use a Purchase Receipt. This keeps stock value and expenses from double-counting the same purchase.
Booking and paying are two different things. An expense invoice always credits the supplier's account. Paying it is a separate step (or the Is Paid tick for genuine over-the-counter cash). The old system mixed these together, which is why cash-paid expenses never appeared in the supplier ledger.

Your reports

Five reports, each answering one question. Knowing which is which saves most of the time people spend hunting.

Stock BalanceHow much of each item do I have, and what is it worth?A customer asks if you have something

Do not ask Stock Balance a money question. It values stock, not debt. "Who owes us?" is Receivables Summary. Mixing the two is the most common reason a figure looks wrong.

Every report is filtered to your branch, exactly like your lists. Set the date range at the top before reading anything — reports open on a default range that may not be the one you want.

Not switched on yet

Three things are deliberately incomplete. They are listed here so nobody wastes time looking for them.

FeatureStateWhat it means for you
ZATCA e-invoicing Installed, not onboarded Invoices work normally but carry no QR code and are not reported to ZATCA. The Sales Invoice print format is intentionally left unset until this is switched on, so nobody starts handing out a layout that has to change.
Automatic item codes Mechanism live, no prefixes set The system can build item codes per item group, but no group has a prefix yet. Type item codes by hand for now.
Closed periods No freeze dates set Nothing stops a document being posted into an old month. Take the posting date seriously — it is not being checked for you.
Before ZATCA is switched on, know this. Once ZATCA Phase 2 is live, a submitted Sales Invoice cannot be cancelled at all. Corrections become credit-note-only. Anyone used to cancelling a mistake needs to change habits before that day, not after.
